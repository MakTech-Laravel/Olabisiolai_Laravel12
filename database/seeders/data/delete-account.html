DELETE YOUR GIDIRA ACCOUNT

This page explains how to delete your Gidira account and what happens to your data after deletion.

Delete your account in the app or website

Log in to your Gidira account, open Account settings, and choose Delete account.

To confirm deletion, type DELETE and enter your password. Once confirmed, your account is permanently deleted immediately.

Delete your account by email

If you cannot access your account, email hello@gidira.com from the email address registered on your account and use the subject line Account deletion.

We may ask for additional information to verify the request before processing it. Verified requests are processed within 30 days.

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile
  • Your business listings and related business page content
  • Your in-app messages, reviews, and wallet records tied directly to your account
  • Your active OAuth access tokens

What may be retained

  • Records required for legal or regulatory compliance
  • Payment or dispute records required to prevent fraud or complete audits
  • Messages sent through the public contact form that are not stored as part of your login account

Need help?

If you need help deleting your account, contact hello@gidira.com.
